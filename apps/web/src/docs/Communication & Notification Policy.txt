Communication & Notification Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 05 August 2026
Last Updated: 05 August 2026

1. Introduction
This Communication & Notification Policy ("Policy") explains how Finbrella communicates with users regarding their accounts, WealthMap, Financial Workspace, financial planning activities, products, services, and other platform-related matters.
The purpose of this Policy is to help users understand:
•	Why Finbrella communicates with them.
•	The different types of communications they may receive.
•	The channels used for communication.
•	How communication preferences may be managed.
•	The circumstances in which certain communications cannot be disabled.
This Policy should be read together with Finbrella's Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and other applicable policies.

2. Purpose of Communications
Finbrella communicates with users to support the effective, secure, and continuous use of the Services.
Communications may be necessary to:
•	Deliver WealthMap and Financial Workspace services.
•	Notify users about important account activity.
•	Inform users of financial planning progress.
•	Remind users to review or update financial information.
•	Support insurance and investment planning activities.
•	Provide educational financial content.
•	Maintain platform security.
•	Comply with legal or regulatory obligations.
•	Improve user experience.
•	Inform users about new features, products, or services.
Finbrella aims to ensure that communications are timely, relevant, and respectful of user preferences wherever reasonably possible.

5. Consent and Communication Preferences
Where required by applicable law, Finbrella will obtain appropriate consent before sending communications that require consent.
Users may manage available communication preferences through account settings or other mechanisms provided by Finbrella.
Depending on the nature of the communication, users may be able to choose:
•	Preferred communication channels.
•	Types of notifications.
•	Educational communications.
•	Promotional communications.
•	Browser notification permissions.
Certain operational, security, legal, or transactional communications form part of the Services and may not be disabled where they are necessary for the protection of users, the operation of the platform, or compliance with applicable legal obligations.

8. Notification Frequency
Finbrella aims to communicate responsibly and avoid excessive notifications.
To promote a positive user experience, Finbrella may apply reasonable limits to the number and frequency of notifications sent to users.
Notification frequency may vary depending on:
•	Account activity.
•	User preferences.
•	Financial planning events.
•	Security requirements.
•	Product updates.
•	Legal or regulatory obligations.
Where practical, Finbrella seeks to prioritise quality and relevance over quantity.

9. Quiet Hours
To minimise unnecessary interruptions, Finbrella may apply quiet hours during which non-essential notifications are deferred.
Examples of communications that may be deferred include:
•	Educational messages.
•	Financial planning reminders.
•	Product announcements.
•	Promotional communications.
However, security alerts, critical account notifications, legal notices, fraud prevention messages, or other important operational communications may still be delivered where reasonably necessary.
Quiet hours may vary based on operational requirements, user preferences, or future platform enhancements.

•	Financial reports.
•	Uploaded documents.
•	Account settings.
•	Other relevant platform features.

12. Testing and Continuous Improvement
Finbrella continuously reviews and improves its communication practices.

Where supported by the Services, users may manage available communication preferences through their Finbrella account settings.

•	Enable or disable browser push notifications.
•	Manage educational communications.
•	Manage promotional communications.
•	Update communication channels.

•	Modify other available communication preferences.

Changes to communication preferences may not affect notifications that have already been scheduled or communications that are legally or operationally required.

Users may opt out of certain non-essential communications where such options are made available.
Examples may include:
•	Promotional communications.
•	Educational campaigns.
•	Product announcements.
•	Marketing-related messages.
However, users generally cannot opt out of communications that are necessary for:
•	Account security.
•	Identity verification.
•	Fraud prevention.
•	Password recovery.
•	Service operation.
•	Legal compliance.
•	Important account administration.
These communications are considered an integral part of providing the Services safely and responsibly.

15. Delivery of Communications
While Finbrella makes reasonable efforts to deliver communications promptly and reliably, successful delivery depends on various factors beyond our direct control.
These factors may include:
•	Internet connectivity.
•	Browser settings.
•	Device settings.
•	Notification permissions.
•	Email service availability.
•	Mobile network availability.
•	Third-party communication providers.
•	Spam filtering.
•	Technical interruptions.
Accordingly, Finbrella cannot guarantee that every communication will be delivered immediately or received by every user.
Users are encouraged to periodically access WealthMap and Financial Workspace directly rather than relying solely on notifications.
