1. Introduction
This Data Retention & Deletion Policy ("Policy") explains how Finbrella retains, manages, archives, and deletes information collected through its Services.
The purpose of this Policy is to provide transparency regarding the lifecycle of information stored within Finbrella, including personal information, financial information, uploaded documents, Generated Financial Outputs, and other data associated with your account.
This Policy should be read together with Finbrella's Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
________________________________________
2. Purpose of Data Retention
Finbrella retains information for legitimate business, operational, security, legal, regulatory, and user experience purposes.
Appropriate data retention enables Finbrella to:
•	Provide WealthMap and Financial Workspace services.
•	Preserve historical financial information and planning records.
•	Track long-term financial progress.
•	Generate meaningful trend analysis.
•	Maintain continuity of financial planning.
•	Protect user accounts.
•	Detect fraud or misuse.
•	Resolve disputes.
•	Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
•	Improve platform performance and user experience.
Finbrella does not retain information for longer than is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

5. Retention of Active Accounts
While your account remains active, Finbrella generally retains information associated with your account in order to:
•	Deliver WealthMap and Financial Workspace services.
•	Maintain historical financial records.
•	Track financial progress over time.
•	Generate trend analysis and comparisons.
•	Enable long-term financial planning.
•	Improve planning accuracy based on updated information.
•	Support insurance review and financial planning features.
•	Provide customer support.
•	Maintain platform security.
Historical information forms an important part of WealthMap's long-term planning experience.
Accordingly, information associated with an active account is ordinarily retained for as long as your account remains active, unless deletion is requested or retention is no longer necessary.

6. Account Deletion and Deactivation
Users may request deletion of their Finbrella account in accordance with the options made available through the Services or by contacting Finbrella.
When an account deletion request is received, Finbrella may first place the account into a deactivated state.
During this period:
•	The user will generally no longer be able to access the account.
•	The account will no longer be available for normal use.
•	Personal Data will no longer be actively processed except where necessary for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, operational continuity, or restoration if requested by the user during the applicable retention period.
The purpose of this deactivation period is to:
•	Prevent accidental loss of long-term financial history.
•	Allow users an opportunity to restore their account if the deletion request was made unintentionally.
•	Protect the integrity of historical financial planning records during the applicable retention period.
Finbrella may retain deactivated account information for a reasonable period before permanent deletion, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, security, and operational requirements.
Once the applicable retention period expires and permanent deletion has been completed, restoration of the account and its historical information may no longer be possible. If the user creates a new account thereafter, it will generally be treated as a new Financial Workspace without access to previously deleted account data.

10. Backup, Archival and Disaster Recovery
To support platform reliability, business continuity, and disaster recovery, Finbrella may maintain secure backup copies of certain information.
Backup copies are maintained solely for legitimate operational purposes, including:
•	Disaster recovery.
•	System restoration.
•	Security investigations.
•	Data integrity verification.
•	Business continuity.
Backup information is subject to appropriate security measures and is not ordinarily used for routine operational processing.
Where technically feasible and legally appropriate, deleted information will also be removed from backup systems in accordance with Finbrella's backup management processes. Due to the nature of backup systems, this may not occur immediately.

11. Legal and Regulatory Retention
Certain information may be retained beyond normal operational periods where required or permitted by applicable law.
Examples include retention necessary for:
•	Compliance with legal or regulatory obligations.
•	Court orders or lawful directions.
•	Taxation or accounting requirements.
•	Fraud prevention and security investigations.
•	Dispute resolution.
•	Exercise or defence of legal claims.
•	Compliance with financial sector obligations.
•	Other lawful business purposes.
Where such obligations apply, Finbrella may retain only the information reasonably necessary for those purposes.
________________________________________
12. Secure Deletion Practices
When information is no longer required for the purposes for which it was collected, and no legal or regulatory obligation requires continued retention, Finbrella will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise that information.
Depending on the nature of the information and the systems involved, deletion may include:
•	Permanent removal from active systems.
•	Secure deletion of uploaded documents.
•	Removal from production databases.
•	Scheduled deletion from backup systems, where applicable.
•	Irreversible anonymisation where continued analytical use is appropriate and permitted by applicable law.
Deletion processes may occur over a reasonable period as part of normal operational and security procedures.

14. Partial Deletion Requests

•	Uploaded documents.
•	Financial records.
•	Profile information.
•	Contact information.
•	Other user-provided information.
Finbrella will evaluate such requests in accordance with applicable law, platform functionality, and any legitimate need to retain information for legal, security, fraud prevention, or operational purposes.

•	Financial reporting and accounting.

•	Security monitoring.
